💠 Why water – and why infused?

Tattava blends science and traditional wisdom: the right water, infused with targeted botanicals, supports your body’s natural rhythms. Choose infusions by season, symptom, and goal; track your intake; and balance the five elements.

  • Hydration is not just volume – it’s timing, temperature, and trace phytonutrients.
  • Infusions are gentle, food‑grade strategies – not substitutes for medical care.
  • AI heuristics suggest balanced mixes; you can fine‑tune by listening to your body.

🛡️ Safety

Pregnant, nursing, or on medication? Consult your clinician before strong herbs/spices. Start mild; observe your body. Avoid infusions that aggravate reflux, allergies, or interact with medicines. Hydration complements, not replaces, therapy.
